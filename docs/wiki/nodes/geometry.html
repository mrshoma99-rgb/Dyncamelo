BoundingBox.PlanGap

The widest strip of open floor between an inner box (equipment) and the outer box (opening) in plan — the 'space between the equipment and the opening edge'. Returns the largest of the four horizontal side gaps; 0 when the equipment reaches every edge. Threshold it to flag openings that need a handrail.

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
outer requiredBoundingBox—The opening's bounding box.
inner requiredBoundingBox—The equipment's bounding box.
OutputTypeWhat it gives
gapnumberThe largest horizontal clear gap, in the same units as the boxes.

gap clearance opening edge plan space perimeter handrail
