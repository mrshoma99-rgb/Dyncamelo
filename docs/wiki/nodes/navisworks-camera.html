Writing your own nodes

Navisworks: Camera

7 nodes. Every node has its own link: click its name. Types ending in [] are lists; an input with a default is optional.

Camera.Current

The current camera position, focal distance and vertical field height.

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputType
positionPoint
focalDistancenumber
heightFieldnumber

Returns: Camera position, focal distance (null when unset) and vertical field height.

camera current position view eye

Camera.LookAt

Moves the camera to 'eye' looking at 'target' (up stays +Z).

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
eye requiredany—Camera position: a Point, or a list of three numbers.
target requiredany—Point to look at: a Point, or a list of three numbers.
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputTypeWhat it gives
donebooleanTrue when the camera was moved.

camera lookat aim point view

Camera.SetFieldOfView

Sets the camera's vertical field of view in degrees (perspective camera) — smaller = more zoomed/telephoto, larger = wider.

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
degrees requirednumber—Vertical field of view in degrees (typical 20–90). Applies to the perspective camera.
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputTypeWhat it gives
donebooleanTrue when the field of view was set.

camera fov field of view lens zoom angle wide

Camera.SetProjection

Switches the camera between perspective (true) and orthographic (false) projection.

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
perspective requiredboolean—True for perspective, false for orthographic.
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputTypeWhat it gives
donebooleanTrue when the projection was set.

camera projection perspective orthographic ortho parallel

Camera.SetStandardView

Sets the camera to a standard view (top, bottom, front, back, left, right, iso) and frames the given items — or the whole model when none are wired. Convention: Z is up and +Y is north, so top is a plan with north at the top of the screen, front looks north from the south side, left looks east from the west side, and iso looks from the south-east above. Assumes a Z-up model; for others use Camera.LookAt.

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
viewstring"iso"One of top, bottom, front, back, left, right, iso.
items any number of wiresModelItem[]nullThe items to frame (leave unwired to frame the whole model).
paddingFactornumber1.2Space to leave around the framed box (1 = tight fit).
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputTypeWhat it gives
viewpointViewpointA copy of the new current viewpoint (feed it to nodes that take a viewpoint, or save it with Viewpoint.SaveCurrent).

camera standard view top plan front back left right iso isometric elevation

Camera.ZoomToItems

Frames the given items in the current view (per-item close-ups, screenshot staging).

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
items any number of wires requiredModelItem[]—The model items to frame.
paddingFactornumber1.5How much space to leave around the items (1 = tight fit).
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputTypeWhat it gives
donebooleanTrue when the camera was moved.

camera zoom frame fit items focus

Viewpoint.SetSectionBox

Applies a section box around a region on the current view (Sectioning > Box, scriptable) — chain ModelItem.BoundingBox for the clash-viewpoint close-up look. enabled=false turns sectioning off.

InputTypeDefaultWhat it does
boundingBox requiredany—The box region, in document units (e.g. from ModelItem.BoundingBox or BoundingBox.ByCorners). Ignored when enabled is false.
enabledbooleantrueTrue to section to the box, false to disable sectioning.
documentDocumentnullThe document (defaults to the active document).
OutputTypeWhat it gives
donebooleanTrue when the clip planes were applied to the current view.

section box clip sectioning viewpoint crop isolate
